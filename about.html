Our Story

Four Decades of
Festive Joy

Vendhan Crackers began as a small family shop in Chennai in with a simple mission — to bring safe, high-quality fireworks to every Indian home. What started as a modest storefront has grown into one of the region's most trusted cracker retailers.

Today, we serve over 50,000 happy families every festival season, offering more than 200 carefully curated products — from traditional sparklers to premium gift boxes. Our commitment to quality, safety and customer satisfaction has never wavered.
